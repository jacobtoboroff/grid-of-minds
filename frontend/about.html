About Grid of Minds

Grid of Minds is a family of free daily trivia puzzle games. Each day, a new 3×3 grid is generated with clues built from real facts — no two days ever repeat the same puzzle. Right now there are four grids to play:

  • Presidential Grid — match U.S. presidents using clues on party, era, service, and more.
  • Shakespeare Grid — match Shakespeare's plays using clues on genre, setting, characters, and more.
  • Authors Grid — match classic novels to the author who wrote them.
  • Elements Grid — match chemical elements using clues from the periodic table.

Your goal in each one? Fill the grid using logic, memory, and deduction — with just 9 guesses. It's like Wordle meets trivia night, crafted for history buffs, literature lovers, science nerds, students, and casual puzzlers alike.

Who Created It?

Grid of Minds was created by a college student passionate about history, literature, and creative gameplay. The idea was to make learning fun, accessible, and a little addictive — one small daily puzzle at a time. As the collection has grown from a single presidential grid into a whole family of games, the goal has stayed the same: make knowledge feel like a game worth playing every day.
