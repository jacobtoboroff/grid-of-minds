⚖️ How to Play Supreme Court Grid

  • Click on a cell in the 2×2 grid to submit a guess that matches the criteria for that row and column.
  • You have 4 guesses total to complete the entire grid.
  • Each guess counts, whether it's right or wrong.
  • You can click any cell in any order.
  • Each guess must be the name of a landmark U.S. Supreme Court case (e.g. "Brown v. Board of Education").
  • Once all 4 cells are filled correctly — you win!
