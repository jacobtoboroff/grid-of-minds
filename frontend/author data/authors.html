📚 How to Play Classic Authors Grid

  • Click on a cell in the grid to submit a guess that matches the criteria for that row and column.
  • Each row is a classic author. Each column is a letter range for the novel's title.
  • You have 6 guesses total to complete the entire grid.
  • Each guess counts, whether it's right or wrong.
  • You can click any cell in any order.
  • Each guess must be the title of a novel written by that row's author, and its title must start with a letter in that column's range.
  • Leading articles ("A," "An," "The") don't count as a title's first letter — e.g. "The Shining" counts as S.
  • Once all 6 cells are filled correctly — you win!

Tip: Think carefully — every guess matters!
